## Rules to avoid getting banned

1. **Read the group rules first** — most groups have pinned rules
2. **Don't post the same message to multiple groups** — Telegram detects cross-posting
3. **Engage genuinely** — reply to other people's questions before promoting your own thing
4. **Wait 24-48h between promotional messages** — don't spam
5. **If a group has a #showcase or #promotions channel, use that** — not the main chat
6. **Never DM group members without permission** — that's reportable
7. **If someone asks you to stop, stop** — don't argue

## What NOT to do

- ❌ Join 20 groups in 1 day and post the same message — Telegram will flag you
- ❌ Use a bot to auto-join and auto-post — bots can't join groups alone, and auto-posting is spam
- ❌ Post links without context — looks like spam
- ❌ Argue with moderators — accept their rules
- ❌ Cross-post the exact same text — even if you change one word, Telegram fingerprinting catches it

## Recommended approach

1. Join 3-5 groups MAX in the first week
2. Spend the first week just reading — understand the culture
3. Start replying to other people's questions (without mentioning UTA)
4. After 1-2 weeks of genuine participation, mention UTA naturally in a relevant conversation
5. If people are interested, they'll ask follow-up questions
6. Only THEN share the repo link

This is slower than spam, but it's how you build real adoption.
